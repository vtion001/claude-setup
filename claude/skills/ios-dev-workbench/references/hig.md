## Key constants every iOS audit knows by heart

- **Minimum tap target**: 44×44 pt (HIG Accessibility section)
- **Body type baseline**: 17 pt (small) / 22 pt (large) at default Dynamic Type
- **Standard margins**: 16 pt phone, 20 pt iPad
- **Modal sheet detents**: `.medium`, `.large`, `.fraction(0.x)`
- **Animation timing**: 0.2–0.5s for most transitions; 0.3s is the workhorse spring
- **Spring response**: 0.4–0.6s response, 0.7–0.85 damping for natural feel
