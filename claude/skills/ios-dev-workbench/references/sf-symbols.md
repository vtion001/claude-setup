## Rendering modes (iOS 15+)
- **Monochrome** — `.imageScale(.large).foregroundColor(.pukaGreen)`
- **Hierarchical** — `.symbolRenderingMode(.hierarchical).foregroundColor(...)`
- **Palette** — `.symbolRenderingMode(.palette).foregroundStyle(..., ..., ...)`
- **Multicolor** — `.symbolRenderingMode(.multicolor)`

## Animations (iOS 17+)
- `.symbolEffect(.bounce, value: state)`
- `.symbolEffect(.pulse)`
- `.symbolEffect(.variableColor.iterative)`
- `.symbolEffect(.appear/.disappear)`

## Audit rule of thumb

If a custom icon image exists and SF Symbols has an equivalent, the
audit recommends migration. Brand mascots / illustrations stay custom.
